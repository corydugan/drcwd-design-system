2 · The surface layer added 5 Aug 2026

Two peer surfaces. Not a light mode and a dark mode.

A component never hard-codes a colour. It reads --surface-* and inherits whichever surface it was placed on. The surface is set on the card root.

Squared by default, because the cards are editorial. Soft chrome is for interactive elements and data tiles only. Hairlines do the heavy lifting at 1px, strong at 2px. Shadows stay extremely restrained: cards lean on borders, not shadow.

The motif

Hairline rules as structure, whitespace at 35 to 40 percent, squared editorial cards, serif-italic grape numerals. On top: evidence on display. Tabular stat blocks as hero elements, a small-caps method label above the data, a thin grape data-baseline rule under the stat, and a citation style that makes the rigour visible.

Voice

First person singular. Australian spelling in this lane. Authoritative, evidence-precise, plain-confident, and it cites the number.

Do
  • "One in three women is iron deficient, and here is what the data shows."
  • Put a figure or a source behind every claim.
  • Use a comma, parentheses, or a full stop.
Never
  • Em dashes. Anywhere, on any surface, including filenames.
  • Emoji. Exclamation marks.
  • Hedging, hype, diet-culture framing, or a claim with no number.
